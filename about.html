Our Story

Motherland Academy Nabweru is a leading educational institution in Uganda, dedicated to providing a holistic learning experience. We offer a robust curriculum that balances both Arts and Sciences, ensuring our students are prepared for the competitive world.

Our Mission

To nurture creative, responsible, and qualified citizens through quality education and discipline.

Our Vision

To be the leading center of academic excellence and moral uprightness in the region.

Why Motherland Academy?

  • Qualified Staff: We have a team of well-trained and dedicated teachers.
  • Top Facilities: Our school features a well-stocked library and modern science laboratories.
  • Spiritual Growth: Religious activities are a core part of our student life.
  • Extracurriculars: We excel in Music, Dance, and Drama (MDD) competitions.
